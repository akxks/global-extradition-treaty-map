This dataset documents 4 extradition or surrender partners for Liberia. These agreements provide legal frameworks for requesting a person's surrender, subject to the applicable laws and restrictions. The inventory is incomplete; an unlisted country is not evidence of no treaty.

The recorded network includes 4 bilateral treaty partners. Categories overlap. Of 4 documented partner jurisdictions, 4 are UN member or observer states used in the network rankings.
