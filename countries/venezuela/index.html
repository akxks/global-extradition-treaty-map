This dataset documents 8 extradition or surrender partners for Venezuela. These agreements provide legal frameworks for requesting a person's surrender, subject to the applicable laws and restrictions. The inventory is incomplete; an unlisted country is not evidence of no treaty.

The recorded network includes 3 bilateral treaty partners; 5 multilateral convention partners. Categories overlap. Of 8 documented partner jurisdictions, 8 are UN member or observer states used in the network rankings.

Research is partial. No complete bilateral and multilateral treaty inventory is certified. Unknown relationships are not findings that extradition is unavailable.

Recorded legal frameworks

Inter-American Convention on Extradition (5 recorded partners); Treaty on extradition between Australia and the Republic of Venezuela (1 recorded partners); Tratado de Extradição entre o Brasil e a Venezuela (1 recorded partners).

These are evidenced legal bases, not assurances of extradition. Conditions, reservations and domestic law must be checked for the particular request.

Inter-American Convention on Extradition

Creates an extradition obligation between ratifying states, subject to conditions and exceptions. It does not automatically replace earlier treaties (Article 33). Only deposited ratifications or accessions are included; other signatures alone do not create coverage.
