This dataset documents 54 extradition or surrender partners for Türkiye. These agreements provide legal frameworks for requesting a person's surrender, subject to the applicable laws and restrictions. The inventory is incomplete; an unlisted country is not evidence of no treaty.

The recorded network includes 4 bilateral treaty partners; 50 multilateral convention partners. Categories overlap. Of 54 documented partner jurisdictions, 54 are UN member or observer states used in the network rankings.

Research is partial. No complete bilateral and multilateral treaty inventory is certified. Unknown relationships are not findings that extradition is unavailable.

Recorded legal frameworks

European Convention on Extradition (ETS No. 024) (50 recorded partners); Treaty on extradition between Australia and the Republic of Turkey (1 recorded partners).

These are evidenced legal bases, not assurances of extradition. Conditions, reservations and domestic law must be checked for the particular request.
