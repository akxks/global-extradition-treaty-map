This dataset documents 48 extradition or surrender partners for Canada. These agreements provide legal frameworks for requesting a person's surrender, subject to the applicable laws and restrictions. The inventory is incomplete; an unlisted country is not evidence of no treaty.

The recorded network includes 48 bilateral treaty partners. Categories overlap. Of 48 documented partner jurisdictions, 48 are UN member or observer states used in the network rankings.

Canada’s official treaty register was checked for active, signed, suspended and historical instruments. Amendments do not add partners. Statutory extradition designations are recorded separately as domestic law, not reciprocal treaties. Successor-state and territorial coverage remain incomplete.
