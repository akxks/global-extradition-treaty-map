This dataset documents 6 extradition or surrender partners for Timor-Leste. These agreements provide legal frameworks for requesting a person's surrender, subject to the applicable laws and restrictions. The inventory is incomplete; an unlisted country is not evidence of no treaty.

The recorded network includes 6 multilateral convention partners. Categories overlap. Of 6 documented partner jurisdictions, 6 are UN member or observer states used in the network rankings.

Research is partial. No complete bilateral and multilateral treaty inventory is certified. Unknown relationships are not findings that extradition is unavailable.

Extradition Convention between the Member States of the Community of Portuguese Language Countries

Extradition convention for verified ratifying states. Article 25(1) replaces bilateral extradition provisions between parties. CPLP membership by itself is insufficient: Guinea-Bissau and Equatorial Guinea are not included without verified ratification.
