This dataset documents 52 extradition or surrender partners for Iceland. These agreements provide legal frameworks for requesting a person's surrender, subject to the applicable laws and restrictions. The inventory is incomplete; an unlisted country is not evidence of no treaty.

The recorded network includes 2 bilateral treaty partners; 22 multilateral convention partners; 28 surrender mechanism partners. Categories overlap. Of 52 documented partner jurisdictions, 52 are UN member or observer states used in the network rankings.

Research is partial. No complete bilateral and multilateral treaty inventory is certified. Unknown relationships are not findings that extradition is unavailable.

Recorded legal frameworks

Agreement between the European Union and the Republic of Iceland and the Kingdom of Norway on the surrender procedure between the Member States of the European Union and Iceland and Norway (24 recorded partners); European Convention on Extradition (ETS No. 024) (22 recorded partners); Convention between the Nordic countries on the surrender of persons for criminal offences (Nordic Arrest Warrant) (4 recorded partners).

These are evidenced legal bases, not assurances of extradition. Conditions, reservations and domestic law must be checked for the particular request.
