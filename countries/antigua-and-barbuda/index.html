This dataset documents 7 extradition or surrender partners for Antigua and Barbuda. These agreements provide legal frameworks for requesting a person's surrender, subject to the applicable laws and restrictions. The inventory is incomplete; an unlisted country is not evidence of no treaty.

The recorded network includes 1 bilateral treaty partners; 5 multilateral convention partners; 1 other arrangement partners. Categories overlap. Of 7 documented partner jurisdictions, 7 are UN member or observer states used in the network rankings.

Research is partial. No complete bilateral and multilateral treaty inventory is certified. Unknown relationships are not findings that extradition is unavailable.

Inter-American Convention on Extradition

Creates an extradition obligation between ratifying states, subject to conditions and exceptions. It does not automatically replace earlier treaties (Article 33). Only deposited ratifications or accessions are included; other signatures alone do not create coverage.
