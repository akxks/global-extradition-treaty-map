This dataset documents 17 extradition or surrender partners for Angola. These agreements provide legal frameworks for requesting a person's surrender, subject to the applicable laws and restrictions. The inventory is incomplete; an unlisted country is not evidence of no treaty.

The recorded network includes 1 bilateral treaty partners; 16 multilateral convention partners. Categories overlap. Of 17 documented partner jurisdictions, 17 are UN member or observer states used in the network rankings.

Research is partial. No complete bilateral and multilateral treaty inventory is certified. Unknown relationships are not findings that extradition is unavailable.

Recorded legal frameworks

Protocol on Extradition of the Southern African Development Community (10 recorded partners); Convenção de Extradição entre os Estados Membros da Comunidade dos Países de Língua Portuguesa (6 recorded partners); 中华人民共和国和安哥拉共和国引渡条约 (1 recorded partners).

These are evidenced legal bases, not assurances of extradition. Conditions, reservations and domestic law must be checked for the particular request.

Protocol on Extradition of the Southern African Development Community

An extradition framework between verified parties, applied alongside domestic law and treaty refusal grounds. The dataset includes only parties whose deposits and effective dates are confirmed by the United Nations registration.

The general entry-into-force date and participation dates follow the SADC registration with the United Nations. Some older government summaries give conflicting dates.

Article 19 makes bilateral extradition agreements complementary; the Protocol prevails where provisions conflict. This does not automatically terminate those bilateral treaties.

The 11 included states form a verified subset, not a declaration that all other SADC states are non-parties. Later participation and the 2019 amendment require further depositary research.

Ratification and entry into force are distinct from guaranteed surrender. Domestic law, offence thresholds and refusal grounds apply.
