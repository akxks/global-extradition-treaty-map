Sources and methodology

This independent reference records legal instruments supported by government, treaty depositary and official legislative sources. The reference and research date is 22 October 2026. An access date is not a guarantee that an older government list captures every subsequent change. Individual entries state source dates and known limits.

Records distinguish bilateral treaties, multilateral conventions, binding surrender mechanisms and other arrangements. Blue appears only where this dataset records an applicable basis. Signature alone is insufficient. Suspended, terminated and unverified instruments are excluded from active counts. Membership of a political organisation alone never creates a relationship.

For multilateral instruments, verified parties and effective dates are stored once. Where a newer mechanism replaces an older one for a country pair, the older basis is shown separately as superseded for ordinary requests. Reservations, nationality rules, offence requirements, human-rights safeguards and transitional cases may affect an individual request. No treaty does not mean extradition is impossible; a treaty does not guarantee extradition.

      Map and territorial scope

      The map uses public-domain Natural Earth political boundaries, projected to SVG using Equal Earth. Borders are generalised for a world map; disputed boundaries do not express a position on sovereignty. Small jurisdictions have enlarged location markers and can also be selected by name.

      ISO 3166-1 alpha-2 codes link countries to records. Kosovo uses the user-assigned code XK; unresolved map features use X-prefixed internal identifiers. Separately mapped territories do not inherit treaties from a sovereign state. Colour on a country's combined geometry is a country-level index, not verification of treaty extension to every overseas area. Antarctica is omitted.
