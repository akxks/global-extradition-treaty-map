Research and verification

Research prioritises national treaty registers, justice and foreign-affairs ministries, official legislation, the UN Treaty Collection, Council of Europe Treaty Office and EUR-Lex. Participation must be supported by a ratification, deposit, effective-status or implementation source. Organisation membership alone is insufficient. An official source can establish a relationship while leaving details incomplete.

1,934 — at least one sourced applicable treaty, surrender mechanism or recognised arrangement.

Active, negative and unknown categories partition all possible pairs. “Fully verified” is a separate audit-quality measure, not another category to add to the total. A source access or verification date is not the treaty’s effective date. Old lists keep their source vintage and limitations. The snapshot date does not assert every underlying source was freshly published or independently checked on that date.

Research coverage report

168 jurisdictions have at least one documented active relationship; 74 have none verified yet. There are 529 instrument records, including inactive and domestic-law-only records. 1,931 active pairs are between UN member or observer states. 115 substantive country profiles are published. No country is certified to have a complete network inventory.

Version 3 preserves the original evidence and adds targeted Canadian, African and Arab-region research plus nationality-law profiles. Additional positive evidence does not resolve every unknown relationship involving those countries.

  • Every country is selectable, but worldwide treaty research is incomplete. No country is certified as exhaustively researched; blue relationships are positive findings from the cited sources.
  • Significant gaps remain in bilateral networks outside the United States, United Kingdom, China, India, Australia and Brazil, especially Africa, the Middle East, Central Asia and small jurisdictions.
  • ECOWAS, SADC, Arab League, CIS and Mercosur instruments have not been expanded without a completed depositary, succession and applicability audit. Organisation membership is never used as a substitute for ratification.
  • Offence-specific UN conventions, all national reservations, additional protocols, territorial extensions, practical suspensions and transitional cases are not comprehensively encoded.
  • Some official inventories are older than October 2026. The date of research is an access date; it is not certification that every subsequent legal development has been captured.
  • Where an official full title or an exact date has not been verified, the record says so. Descriptive labels are not presented as official treaty names.
  • Domestic legislation enabling one-way extradition is documented in relationship details but does not create a blue treaty relationship on its own.
  • SADC expansion is restricted to 11 parties with United Nations deposit and effective-date evidence. Current later-party deposits, amendment participation and national implementation remain incomplete. Zimbabwe’s 2021 state report and Mozambique-related case law are leads, not yet incorporated into the multilateral roster.
  • The Riyadh Agreement expansion contains 16 explicitly listed ratifying states. The government-hosted list is historical and undated; further participants, individual effective dates, current reservations and amendment applicability require depositary confirmation.
  • ECOWAS Convention A/P.1/8/94 text is verified, but a complete reliable deposit/entry-into-force roster and the effects of subsequent ECOWAS withdrawals were not established. No all-ECOWAS pair expansion is made.
  • CIS Minsk/Chisinau and other Arab/African instruments are still unverified in this expansion; regional organisation membership is not treated as treaty participation.
  • Five Somalia bilateral treaty relationships are positively documented by the 2025 UNODC review, but original bilateral texts, official titles and dates remain unrecovered; these records visibly retain missing fields.
  • Official treaty titles and identifiers are taken from Global Affairs Canada. The page-template Date modified footer is not used as the legal status date. Each record states when it was consulted.
  • An instrument amendment is shown for research depth but partner counts must deduplicate countries.
  • Current in-force status is sourced; this is not a certificate that every legal condition, reservation, amendment and domestic rule has been independently audited. No relationship in this expansion is labelled comprehensively verified.
  • No jurisdiction has a certified complete inventory; fully audited pair reviews are not yet complete. Positive sourced applicability and exhaustive legal verification are different measures.
  • Nationality profiles cover 13 jurisdictions. All other nationality rules remain unverified; France and the United States have explicitly documented current-source access limitations.

Relationship classifications and counting

  • Bilateral treaty: a treaty between two states, including a documented inherited or successor-state basis.
  • Multilateral convention: a verified instrument and applicable participation, stored once with member dates. Cross-group agreements apply only across their stated groups.
  • Separate surrender mechanism: for example the European Arrest Warrant, Nordic warrant or EU–UK surrender framework.
  • Other arrangement: a separately sourced extradition route. A one-way domestic designation alone is not treated as a reciprocal treaty.
  • Verified absence: a positively supported conclusion within its stated scope. It is never inferred from an empty record.
  • Unknown: insufficient information, including signature without verified entry into force.

Suspended, terminated, superseded and signed-only records do not add active partners. Where a newer route replaces the ordinary application of an older convention, the older record is retained in relationship details. Historical supplements do not add extra partners. Offence-limited arrangements are labelled and are not presented as unrestricted extradition rights.

Ranking and comparison methodology

Each of the 193 UN members and two observer states is compared with the other 194. A partner counts once if at least one applicable basis exists. This consistent inclusion rule is not a judgment on disputed sovereignty. Non-state territories and separately mapped jurisdictions are excluded from ranking partner counts. Category counts can overlap and must not be added together.

Rankings describe documented legal networks, not actual enforcement, successful requests or practical power. Definitive rankings require a certified complete inventory, a review of all 194 other states and no unresolved state relationships. No country currently qualifies. The largest-network table is explicitly provisional; the definitive smallest-network table is empty. The optional smallest documented-count table excludes countries with no active evidence and is explicitly a research-coverage view. Unknown never becomes zero legal relationships. Equal counts share competition ranks (1, 1, 3).

Comparisons use the same rules. Shared and unique lists exclude the two selected subjects; mutual relationships still count in each total. “Only documented for” means the other country lacks positive evidence in this dataset, not that it lacks an agreement.

Simple and detailed legal information

Country summaries explain the recorded facts in ordinary language. Treaty lists show instrument names and status. Expandable legal records add party dates, identifiers, ratification, reservations, territorial scope, domestic legislation and sources where verified. “Not reviewed” is an explicit gap, not an assertion that no restriction exists. Citizenship summaries address requests to the nationality state; they do not establish protection while abroad. Dual nationality, residence, offence date and the particular route can matter.

Map, borders and territorial scope

Public-domain Natural Earth 1:50m political boundaries are projected to SVG with Equal Earth. Small jurisdictions have enlarged markers. Boundaries are generalised and disputed features express no view on sovereignty. Antarctica is omitted.

ISO alpha-2 codes link records and shapes; Kosovo uses XK and unresolved features use X-prefixed identifiers. Dependencies do not inherit treaties automatically. Combined country geometry is a country-level index, not verified treaty extension to every overseas area.

Green identifies the selection; blue an applicable bilateral or multilateral treaty; purple a separate surrender mechanism or other arrangement where no formal treaty record also applies; grey a sourced negative within a stated scope; yellow insufficient evidence. Status labels, the searchable selector and tables supplement colour.

Country detection and privacy

The page renders before a non-blocking request to /country.json. Netlify’s country-based CDN routing selects a static JSON file containing only an approximate country code. There is no application backend, API key, GPS request, third-party geolocation service or application storage of IP addresses. Netlify processes connection information as the hosting provider; normal hosting logs and its privacy terms still apply.

A supported country or comparison URL takes precedence over saved choices and detection. A manually selected country is saved locally until the detection reset is used. VPNs, mobile networks and incomplete host location data can be inaccurate. Failure leaves the selector usable. On a generic static host, the endpoint returns an unknown-country fallback.

Only country and appearance preferences are stored in local storage. Passport selections stay in memory. The website has no analytics scripts or advertising trackers. Clicking external sources or sponsor links visits their own services and privacy policies.

Appearance and accessibility

Light, dark and automatic appearances use CSS variables informed by Wikimedia Codex colour tokens and its dark-mode definitions. Automatic follows the device preference. Map category hues retain a consistent legend in both modes. This is an independent site, with no Wikimedia affiliation.

Corrections and reproducibility

Report a data error on GitHub with the countries, record, official source and relevant dates. Reports are reviewed against primary evidence before publication; user submissions never change the dataset automatically. Git history preserves changes. Please do not submit personal case details.
